Highlights

- Trajectory estimation of a Venus aerobot during a 65 h Deep Space Network blackout
- An attitude MEKF and a position particle filter are coupled in cascade
- Uncertainties exchanged between the filters are re-injected as measurement noise
- A microwave Sun sensor resolves the longitude ambiguity of terrain navigation
- Mean position RMSE of 26.7 km over 46 h of sunlit autonomous flight
